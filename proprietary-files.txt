# Blobs for the Y220-U05 on CM10.1, from two stock systems:
#
#   y220: the phone's own GB stock (C482B206SP05), as extracted into the
#         CM7 vendor tree (vendor/huawei/y220 branch master).
#   y330: Huawei Y330-U01 V100R001C00B121, Android 4.2.2, MT6572 ALPS.JB3.
#         Same SoC, same Mali r3p2-01rel0 and kernel 3.4.5 as the Y220.
#
# Format: <source>:<path in that system>[:<path in /system>]
# extract-files.py copies them to vendor/huawei/y220/proprietary and writes
# y220-vendor-blobs.mk.
#
# Rule for picking a source: the 4.2 userspace comes from the Y330. What
# talks to the Y220's own modem or carries its board tuning comes from the
# Y220: modem image, ccci daemons, combo firmware, thermal tables.
#
# NVRAM: the AP side (nvram_daemon, libnvram, libcustom_nvram) is the
# Y330's. Its audio and BT blobs pass LIDs compiled from the Y330 table,
# which has two more common entries than the Y220's, so every audio LID is
# off by two against the Y220 libcustom_nvram. The modem side (ccci_fsd,
# IMEI) stays on the Y220 binaries.
